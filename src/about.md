# About

I am a systems programmer and security engineer working at the
intersection of low-level software, network protocols, and
vulnerability research.

My work focuses on understanding how systems actually behave under
real conditions --- through reverse engineering, protocol analysis,
and adversarial testing --- then feeding that understanding back into
tooling and upstream improvements.

---

## Focus Areas

- Low-level systems (Linux, kernel interfaces, performance tuning)
- Network protocols and traffic analysis
- Vulnerability research and exploit development
- Security tooling and automation

---

## Approach

- Reverse engineering over assumptions
- Reproducible research and verifiable results
- Upstream-first contributions where possible
- Minimal, composable tooling
